Annotated Bibliography

Technical Implementation

Alfaiz, N. S., & Fati, S. M. (2022). Enhanced credit card fraud detection model using machine learning. Electronics, 11(4), 662. https://doi.org/10.3390/electronics11040662
This research paper provided foundational understanding of machine learning approaches for fraud detection, particularly the comparison of different algorithms and their performance metrics in real-world scenarios.

Chen, Z. (2023). Performance comparison of machine learning algorithms for credit card fraud detection. Applied and Computational Engineering, 6(1), 599-607. https://doi.org/10.54254/2755-2721/6/20230901
Used to benchmark model performance and understand the relative strengths of different algorithms (XGBoost, LightGBM, LSTM) in handling imbalanced fraud detection datasets.

SHAP documentation. (2025). An introduction to explainable AI with Shapley values. SHAP Latest Documentation. https://shap.readthedocs.io/en/latest/example_notebooks/overviews/An%20introduction%20to%20explainable%20AI%20with%20Shapley%20values.html
Primary resource for implementing SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) to make the machine learning model interpretable and explain predictions to stakeholders.

Brownlee, J. (2018). How to develop LSTM models for time series forecasting. MachineLearningMastery.Com. https://www.machinelearningmastery.com/how-to-develop-lstm-models-for-time-series-forecasting/
Guided the implementation of LSTM networks for sequential fraud pattern detection, though ultimately tree-based models proved more effective for this specific dataset.

TensorFlow. (2025). tf.keras.layers.LSTM. TensorFlow v2.16.1 Documentation. https://www.tensorflow.org/api_docs/python/tf/keras/layers/LSTM
Official documentation for implementing LSTM layers in the experimental phase of model development.

MachineLearningMastery. (2025). Time series prediction with LSTM recurrent neural networks in Python with Keras. https://machinelearningmastery.com/time-series-prediction-lstm-recurrent-neural-networks-python-keras/
Provided practical implementation examples for time-series analysis of transaction data patterns.

Nabil, N. (2024). Building a multilingual static website: A step-by-step guide. Medium. https://medium.com/@nohanabil/building-a-multilingual-static-website-a-step-by-step-guide-7af238cc8505
Guided the implementation of the multi-language feature in the educational webpage, ensuring accessibility for diverse user groups.

Domain Knowledge

U.S. Congress. (n.d.). 15 USC Chapter 41, Subchapter I, Part D: Credit billing. United States Code. https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title15-chapter41-subchapter1-partD&edition=prelim
Primary legal reference for understanding consumer rights and bank responsibilities under the Fair Credit Billing Act, forming the basis for the "Your Rights" section of the educational webpage.

Federal Trade Commission. (2013). Fair Credit Billing Act. https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/statutes/fair-credit-billing-act
Provided clear explanations of consumer protections, including the 60-day reporting window and $50 liability limit, which were translated into accessible content for end-users.

Financial Empowerment Network of Washington State. (2025). Consumer protection resources. https://www.fenwa.org/consumer-protection-resources
Key stakeholder resource that informed the design requirements, particularly the need for mobile accessibility and multi-language support for vulnerable communities.

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. (2023). How do I get my money back after I discover an unauthorized transaction or money missing from my bank account? https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-do-i-get-my-money-back-after-i-discover-an-unauthorized-transaction-or-money-missing-from-my-bank-account-en-1017/
Provided step-by-step guidance for fraud resolution that was adapted into actionable resources for the educational webpage.

IdentityTheft.gov. (2025). Identity theft reporting and recovery. https://www.identitytheft.gov
Official government resource linked in the educational materials to help users report identity theft and begin recovery processes.
