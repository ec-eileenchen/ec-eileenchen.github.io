Key Insight: Precision-Recall Tradeoff

The model was optimized for higher recall (62.9%) to catch more fraudulent transactions, accepting lower precision (33.1%) to minimize financial losses. This reflects real-world banking priorities where missing fraud is more costly than occasional false positives.

Business Impact: This strategic balance ensures that genuine fraud cases are detected while maintaining reasonable operational efficiency for financial institutions.
